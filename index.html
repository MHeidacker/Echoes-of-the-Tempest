Game Features

Everything that makes Echoes of the Tempest unique

17 Dungeons

Battle through procedurally generated dungeons from Pirate Cave to The Shatters. Multi-phase boss encounters await.

⚔

23 Classes

Choose from 15 classic classes plus custom classes including Summoner, Templar, Drakzix, Shrine Maiden, Gambler, Blademaster, Spirithunter, and Reaver.

★

Realm Events

Hunt rare realm event bosses across the overworld for mythic and primordial loot.

☰

Gold Economy

Earn gold from defeated enemies and trade through a balanced economy. Harder content yields greater rewards.

Trading

Trade items with other players and build your fortune through the player-driven trading system.

Legend Passives

Mythic and Primordial items feature unique passive abilities that trigger in combat, rewarding skilled play.
