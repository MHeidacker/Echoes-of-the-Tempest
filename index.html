A Realm of the Mad God experience reimagined

Features

5 Custom Dungeons

Battle through the Temple of Storms, Inferno Depths, Glacial Sanctum, Prismatic Paradise, and the Void Abyss. Each dungeon features unique bosses with multi-phase encounters.

12 Playable Classes

Choose from 11 classic classes plus the all-new Summoner class, which channels elemental spirits with a summoning staff and orb.

Realm Events

Hunt 5 rare realm event bosses: The Stormweaver, Infernal Colossus, Frostbane the Eternal, The Void Sentinel, and Celestial Phoenix.

Gold Economy

Earn gold from defeated enemies and trade through a balanced economy. Harder content yields greater rewards.

Marketplace & Crafting

Trade items with other players and craft powerful gear through the forge system.

Skill Tree

Progress through a marks and nodes skill tree system to customize your character beyond base stats.

How to Play

  1. Download the game client (Echoes of the Tempest AIR application)
  2. Launch the executable — no Flash Player required
  3. Create an account and pick your starting class
  4. Enter the realm, defeat enemies, and collect loot
  5. Explore dungeons, fight bosses, and earn legendary gear
