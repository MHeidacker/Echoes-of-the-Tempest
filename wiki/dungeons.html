Dungeons

Guide to all 5 custom dungeons with enemies and loot tables.

Temple of Storms

★★★★★★ Lightning / Storm

A floating temple crackling with perpetual lightning. Navigate electrified corridors while dodging storm-powered enemies, then face Thaleos in a 4-phase battle.

Boss: Thaleos the Stormcaller

Enemies

Storm Acolyte Thunder Elemental Spark Wisp

Loot Drops

Stormcaller's Staff (UT) Tempest Robe (UT) Ring of the Eye (UT) Tempest of Thaleos (LG) Eye of the Primordial Storm (AR)

Inferno Depths

★★★★★★ Fire / Volcanic

Descend into volcanic caverns filled with lava flows and fire enemies. Pyraxis cycles through 4 phases of increasing intensity.

Boss: Pyraxis the Molten King

Enemies

Magma Crawler Infernal Imp Lava Golem

Loot Drops

Flamecaster's Staff (UT) Magma Hide Armor (UT) Cinderstone Ring (UT) Pyraxis's Molten Core (LG) Heart of the Volcano (AR)

Glacial Sanctum

★★★★★★ Ice / Frost

An ancient frozen fortress guarding relics of absolute zero. Ice-themed enemies slow and paralyze. Crythalis commands blizzards across 4 phases.

Boss: Crythalis the Frozen Warden

Enemies

Glacial Sentinel Frost Wraith Blizzard Elemental

Loot Drops

Frostbite Sword (UT) Crystalline Plate (UT) Permafrost Ring (UT) Scepter of Eternal Winter (LG) Glacial Heart of Crythalis (AR)

Prismatic Paradise

★★★★★★ Rainbow / Prismatic

A dazzling realm of rainbow colors reached through the Prismatic Unicorn event. Sparklehorn cycles through 3 phases of increasingly chaotic sparkle attacks.

Boss: Sparklehorn the Magnificent

Enemies

Sparkle Wisp

Loot Drops

Rainbow Wand (LG) Prismatic Crown (AR)

Void Abyss

★★★★★★ Shadow / Void

The deepest void dungeon, accessed through the Void Sentinel realm event. Archdemon Malphas shifts between size forms with devastating multi-phase attacks.

Boss: Archdemon Malphas

Enemies

Malphas Missile

Loot Drops

Demon Blade (UT) Dagger of the Flaming Stinger (UT) Armor from the Bowels of Hell (UT) The Ring of Flaming Power (UT)
