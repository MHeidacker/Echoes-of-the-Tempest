Dungeons

Guide to all 5 custom dungeons with boss phases, enemies, and loot tables.

Temple of Storms

Difficulty:

Theme: Lightning / Storm

A floating temple crackling with perpetual lightning. Navigate electrified corridors while dodging storm-powered enemies, then face Thaleos in a 4-phase battle.

Boss: Thaleos the Stormcaller — 50,000 (ScaleHP: 25,000)

Boss Phases

  1. Phase1_Awakening
  2. Phase2_Tempest
  3. Phase3_Summon
  4. Phase4_Fury

Enemies: Storm Acolyte, Thunder Elemental, Spark Wisp

Loot Drops

  • Stormcaller's Wand (UT)
  • Tempest Robe (UT)
  • Ring of the Eye (UT)
  • Tempest of Thaleos (LG)
  • Eye of the Primordial Storm (AR)

Inferno Depths

Difficulty:

Theme: Fire / Volcanic

Descend into volcanic caverns filled with lava flows and fire enemies. Pyraxis cycles through 4 phases of increasing intensity.

Boss: Pyraxis the Molten King — 35,000 (ScaleHP: 17,500)

Boss Phases

  1. Phase1_Ignite
  2. Phase2_Eruption
  3. Phase3_Summon
  4. Phase4_Enrage

Enemies: Magma Crawler, Infernal Imp, Lava Golem

Loot Drops

  • Flamecaster's Staff (UT)
  • Magma Hide Armor (UT)
  • Cinderstone Ring (UT)
  • Pyraxis's Molten Core (LG)
  • Heart of the Volcano (AR)

Glacial Sanctum

Difficulty:

Theme: Ice / Frost

An ancient frozen fortress guarding relics of absolute zero. Ice-themed enemies slow and paralyze. Crythalis commands blizzards across 4 phases.

Boss: Crythalis the Frozen Warden — 45,000 (ScaleHP: 22,500)

Boss Phases

  1. Phase1_Freeze
  2. Phase2_Blizzard
  3. Phase3_Summon
  4. Phase4_Enrage

Enemies: Frost Wraith, Glacial Sentinel, Blizzard Elemental

Loot Drops

  • Frostbite Dagger (UT)
  • Crystalline Plate (UT)
  • Permafrost Ring (UT)
  • Scepter of Eternal Winter (LG)
  • Glacial Heart of Crythalis (AR)

Prismatic Paradise

Difficulty:

Theme: Rainbow / Prismatic

A dazzling realm of rainbow colors reached through the Prismatic Unicorn event. Sparklehorn cycles through 3 phases of increasingly chaotic sparkle attacks.

Boss: Sparklehorn the Magnificent — 50,000 (ScaleHP: 50,000)

Boss Phases

  1. Frolic
  2. TransDazzle
  3. Dazzle
  4. TransFury
  5. PrismaticFury
  6. Welcome
  7. Phase1
  8. TransPhase2
  9. Phase2
  10. TransPhase3
  11. Phase3

Enemies: Rainbow Imp, Glitter Golem, Sparkle Wisp

Loot Drops

  • Rainbow Wand (LG)
  • Prismatic Crown (AR)

Void Abyss

Difficulty:

Theme: Shadow / Void

The deepest void dungeon, accessed through the Void Sentinel realm event. Archdemon Malphas shifts between size forms with devastating multi-phase attacks.

Boss: Archdemon Malphas — ScaleHP: 30,000

Enemies: Malphas Missile

Loot Drops

  • Demon Blade (UT)
  • Dagger of the Flaming Stinger (UT)
  • Armor from the Bowels of Hell (UT)
  • The Ring of Flaming Power (UT)
