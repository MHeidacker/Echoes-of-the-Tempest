Server Guide

Getting Started

1.

Download the Client

Grab the latest EchoesOfTheTempest.zip from the GitHub Releases page. Extract it anywhere and run EchoesOfTheTempest.exe -- no Flash Player or SDK required.

2.

Create an Account

Register in-game with an email and password. Your account, characters, and items are stored on the server.

3.

Choose a Class

Pick from 23 playable classes -- each with a unique weapon type, ability, and armor class. New players should try Wizard (ranged DPS) or Knight (tanky melee).

4.

Enter the Realm

Start by farming Godlands enemies for gear upgrades and dungeon portal drops. Kill realm event bosses for Mythic and Primordial loot.

5.

Run Dungeons

17 dungeons each drop unique equipment. Boss encounters scale with player count. Progress from Pirate Cave through endgame challenges like The Shatters and Void Abyss.

6.

Hunt Primordial Gear

The rarest items -- Primordial (PR) -- drop from realm event bosses and dungeon bosses at a 1/3400 rate. These build-defining items have unique passive powers that fundamentally change how you play.

Permadeath

When your character dies, they are gone forever -- along with all equipped items and inventory. There are no respawns and no second chances. This is what makes every decision matter.

On death, your character's accumulated experience is converted into Fame -- a permanent account currency. Fame under 200,000 XP is calculated as XP / 1000; above that threshold it scales as 200 + (XP - 200,000) / 1000.

Items stored in your Vault survive death. Smart players bank valuable gear before risky dungeon runs.

Stats

Every character has 8 core stats that grow on level-up (random rolls per class) and can be permanently boosted with stat potions. Each stat has a per-class cap.

HP Hit Points. Reach zero and you die -- permanently.
MP Mana Points. Consumed when using your class ability.
ATT Attack. Increases weapon damage output.
DEF Defense. Reduces incoming damage per hit.
SPD Speed. Movement speed in the world.
DEX Dexterity. Increases attack speed (rate of fire).
VIT Vitality. HP regeneration rate over time.
WIS Wisdom. MP regeneration rate over time.

Item Rarities

Tiered (T1-T14) Common

Standard equipment that drops from most enemies. Higher tiers drop from harder enemies. No special abilities -- pure stat sticks. Tradeable.

Untiered (UT) UT

Unique items with special properties that tiered gear lacks -- unusual projectile patterns, condition effects, or stat tradeoffs. Drop rate: 5% (1/20) from specific bosses. Soulbound. Drops in a cyan bag.

Resonant (RS) RS

Enhanced untiered items that resonate with elemental energy. Stronger base stats than UT with minor passive effects. Drop rate: 1% (1/100) from dungeon bosses. Soulbound. Drops in a cyan bag.

Mythic (MY) MY

Rare items with a Legend passive power -- conditional buffs, proc-on-hit effects, aura abilities, or stacking mechanics that enhance your playstyle. Drop rate: 0.125% (1/800). Soulbound. Drops in a gold bag.

Primordial (PR) PR

The pinnacle. Primordial items don't just buff you -- they change how you play. Frost Wisp companions that fight alongside you. Volcanic auras that melt enemies. Void singularities from your sword strikes. Shadow clones that spawn when you're hit. Each one creates a unique gameplay experience.

Drop rate: 0.03% (1/3400). Soulbound. Drops in a radiant bag with a glowing aura.

Passive Powers (Legend System)

Mythic and Primordial items have Legend passives -- abilities that activate automatically based on different triggers. There are 9 passive types across 40+ items.

Proc-on-Hit

Chance to trigger on each weapon hit. Chain lightning, armor break, lifesteal -- turns every shot into a gamble.

Hit Stack

Build stacks with each hit. At threshold, unleash a powerful burst -- AOE explosions, armor breaks, or stat surges.

Periodic Aura

Pulses on a timer. Damage enemies, heal allies, or apply debuffs in an area around you. Rewards aggressive positioning.

On Ability

Triggers when you use your class ability. Grants buffs like Speedy, Invisible, or stat boosts after each ability use.

On Kill

Activates when you slay an enemy. Berserk frenzy, healing bursts, or stat stacking that rewards clearing fast.

Position Stack

Build stacks by standing still or moving. Grants Armored, DEF boosts, or healing when you hold your ground.

Phase Cycle

Rotates through phases on a timer. Solar/Lunar cycles, prismatic color shifts -- each phase grants different bonuses.

Damage Threshold

Triggers when your HP drops below a percentage. Emergency heals, Armored buff, or retaliatory explosions.

Always Active

Permanent passive effects while equipped. Constant lifesteal, Damaging condition, DEF aura, or HP regeneration. Simple but powerful.

Dungeons

17 procedurally generated dungeons, each with unique enemies, bosses, and loot tables. Dungeon portals drop from realm enemies and event bosses. See the Dungeons page for full details.

Pirate Cave Snake Pit Sprite World Undead Lair Abyss of Demons Mad Lab Ocean Trench Tomb of the Ancients Puppet Theatre Tunnel of Pain The Shatters Temple of Storms Storm Palace Inferno Depths Glacial Sanctum Prismatic Paradise Void Abyss

Dungeon Keys

Consumable items that open a dungeon portal on use. Keys can be purchased with gold or found as rare drops. Using a key in the Nexus creates a portal accessible to all nearby players -- useful for running specific dungeons without waiting for realm drops.

Dungeon Modifiers

Dungeon instances can roll random modifiers that change the difficulty and rewards. Modifiers are displayed when you enter the dungeon.

Fortified Enraged Gilded Cursed Frozen

Harder modifiers increase drop rates. Stacking modifiers multiply the challenge and the reward.

Enchantments

Apply enchantments to equipment for permanent stat bonuses. There are 6 enchantment types across 2 tiers (Standard and Superior). Each enchantment costs gold to apply and is consumed on use.

Might (ATT) Fortitude (DEF) Swiftness (SPD) Precision (DEX) Vitality (VIT) Insight (WIS)

Superior enchantments give larger bonuses but cost significantly more gold. Items can only have one enchantment at a time -- applying a new one replaces the old.

Achievements

30 achievements across 4 categories track your progress and reward you with titles displayed next to your name.

Combat Kills, bosses, damage
Exploration Dungeons, portals, realm
Collection Items, fame, gold
Social Guilds, trades, parties

Guilds & Parties

Parties of up to 6 players share XP, see each other on the minimap, and can teleport to party members. Create or join a party with /party invite <name>.

Guilds are permanent organizations with their own chat channel, a shared Guild Hall safe zone, and a guild leaderboard. Guild creation costs fame. Members share a guild tag displayed next to their name.

World Boss: Typhon

When the realm closes, Typhon appears -- the ultimate world boss. All remaining players are teleported to a dedicated arena for a server-wide battle. Typhon has multiple phases with devastating attacks and massive HP scaling.

Defeating Typhon guarantees high-tier loot for all participants, including a chance at Mythic and Primordial drops. Failing to kill Typhon before the timer expires means everyone dies.
