Combat Mechanics

Reference for damage formulas, fire rate calculations, and status effects.

Damage Formula

Base damage for each projectile hit:

damage = avg(MinDamage, MaxDamage) * (0.5 + ATT / 50) - DEF

Minimum damage is 25% of the pre-DEF value (damage can never go below this floor).

Example: weapon with 80-120 damage (avg 100), 60 ATT, vs 20 DEF:

avg    = (80 + 120) / 2 = 100
scaled = 100 * (0.5 + 60/50) = 100 * 1.7 = 170
final  = 170 - 20 = 150
floor  = 170 * 0.25 = 42.5
result = max(150, 42.5) = 150

Fire Rate Formula

Attacks per second are determined by DEX and the weapon's Rate of Fire multiplier:

frequency = 0.0015 + (DEX / 75) * 0.0065
period    = 1 / frequency / RateOfFire

At 75 DEX with 1.0x RoF: frequency = 0.008, period = 125ms (8 shots/sec).

At 75 DEX with 1.2x RoF: period = ~104ms (9.6 shots/sec).

ScaleHP

Boss HP scales with the number of players in the dungeon:

effective_hp = base_hp + (player_count - 1) * ScaleHP_value

Example: Thaleos has 50,000 base HP and ScaleHP of 25,000. With 4 players: 50,000 + 3 × 25,000 = 125,000 HP.

Status Effects

Slowed Movement speed reduced by 50%
Paralyzed Cannot move (can still shoot and use abilities)
Sick Cannot heal from any source
Armor Broken DEF reduced to 0
Stunned Cannot shoot or use abilities
Confused Movement controls are scrambled
Dazed All attacks deal 50% reduced damage
Quiet Cannot use abilities (MP locked)
Weak Attack damage reduced by 50%
Darkness Vision radius significantly reduced
Armored DEF doubled (buff, used by bosses)
Invulnerable Immune to all damage (phase transitions)
Healing Rapidly regenerate HP over time
Berserk Attack speed increased by 50%
Swiftness Movement speed increased
