Server Guide

Everything you need to know about Echoes of the Tempest — getting started, item rarities, loot bags, and combat mechanics.

Getting Started

1.

Download the Client

Grab the latest EchoesOfTheTempest.zip from the GitHub Releases page. Extract it anywhere and run EchoesOfTheTempest.exe — no Flash Player or SDK required.

2.

Create an Account

Register in-game with an email and password. Your account, characters, and items are stored on the server.

3.

Enter the Realm

Choose a class and enter the realm. Start by farming Godlands enemies for gear upgrades and dungeon portal drops. Kill realm event bosses for Legendary and Exalted loot.

4.

Run Dungeons

Five custom dungeons each drop unique equipment. Boss encounters scale with player count. Clear all five to unlock the best gear in the game.

5.

Hunt Exalted Gear

The rarest items — Exalted (EX) — drop from realm event bosses and dungeon bosses at a 1/3400 rate. These build-defining items have unique passive powers that fundamentally change how you play.

Item Rarities

Tiered (T1-T14) Common

Standard equipment that drops from most enemies. Higher tiers drop from harder enemies. No special abilities — pure stat sticks. Tradeable.

Untiered (UT) UT

Unique items with special properties that tiered gear lacks — unusual projectile patterns, condition effects, or stat tradeoffs. Drop rate: 5% (1/20) from specific bosses. Soulbound. Drops in a cyan bag.

Legendary (LG) LG

Rare items with a Legend passive power — conditional buffs, proc-on-hit effects, aura abilities, or stacking mechanics that enhance your playstyle. Drop rate: 0.125% (1/800). Soulbound. Drops in a cyan bag.

Exalted (EX) EX

The pinnacle. Exalted items don't just buff you — they change how you play. Frost Wisp companions that fight alongside you. Volcanic auras that melt enemies. Void singularities from your sword strikes. Shadow clones that spawn when you're hit. Each one creates a unique gameplay experience.

Drop rate: 0.03% (1/3400). Soulbound. Drops in a radiant bag with a glowing aura.

Loot Bags

When enemies die, they drop loot in colored bags. The bag color is determined by the rarest item inside.

Brown Bag

Basic drops — T0 starter gear, consumables. Public (anyone can loot).

Pink Bag

Low-tier loot — T1-T4 equipment. Public.

Purple Bag

Mid-tier soulbound loot — potions, T5+ gear. Only you can see it.

Blue Bag

Higher-tier drops — T8+ gear, stat potions.

Cyan Bag

Untiered (UT) items. Soulbound — unique equipment with special properties.

Gold Bag

Legendary (LG) items. Soulbound — rare drops with passive Legend powers. Your heart skips a beat.

Radiant Bag

Exalted (EX) items only. Glows with a pulsing radiant aura on the ground. If you see this, screenshot it — it's a 1/3400 drop.

Passive Powers (Legend System)

Legendary and Exalted items have Legend passives — abilities that activate automatically based on different triggers.

Conditional Buff

Activates when a condition is met (below X% HP, Surge above threshold). Grants temporary buffs like Armored, Berserk, or Healing.

Proc-on-Hit

Chance to trigger on each weapon hit. Chain lightning, armor break, pressure stacks, lifesteal — turns every shot into a gamble.

Companion

Summon an entity that fights alongside you. Frost Wisps fire ice shards; Guardian Spirits heal allies. Can sacrifice themselves to save you.

Aura / Proximity

Passive area effects. Damage enemies near you, heal allies, apply debuffs. Rewards aggressive positioning.

Resource Management

Rewrites your HP/MP/Surge economy. MP becomes a shield, Surge fuels eruptions, missing HP increases damage.

Weapon Modification

Changes how your weapon fundamentally works. Shots chain to enemies, leave void rifts, or spawn shadow clones. Unique to Exalted items.
